# Estate Notices, Circulars & Resident Acknowledgements

## Design
- **Audience Targeting**: Notices can target entire estates, specific buildings (Tower A/B), or owner/tenant segments.
- **Read Receipts vs Acknowledgements**: `NoticeReadReceipt` tracks open events; `NoticeAcknowledgement` records explicit resident acceptance.
- **Emergency Broadcasts**: Urgent alerts bypass routine lead times while preserving authoritative database records.
