# Governance Committee & Leadership Tenure Architecture

## Principles
1. **Committees**: Represent functional or statutory bodies (`MANAGING`, `EXECUTIVE`, `FINANCE`, `SECURITY`, `MAINTENANCE`, `CULTURAL`, `ELECTION`).
2. **Terms**: Multi-year elected or appointed periods (e.g. 2026–2029).
3. **Positions**: Distinct governance responsibilities (`President`, `Secretary`, `Treasurer`, `Member`).
4. **Effective-Dated Memberships**: All appointments have explicit `startDate` and `endDate` to ensure historical accuracy over leadership transitions.
