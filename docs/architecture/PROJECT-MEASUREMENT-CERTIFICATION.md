# Measurement Book (MB) & Progress Certification (IPC / RA Bills)

## Measurement Book Foundation
- Records physical on-site quantities completed by contractors before billing.
- Validates $\text{Cumulative Measured} \le \text{Allowed BOQ Quantity}$.
- Verification required by designated Site Engineer.

## Progress Certificates (Interim Payment Certificates)
- Aggregates verified measurements for a billing cycle.
- Calculates gross certified amount based on contracted BOQ rates.
- Automatically computes 5% statutory retention withholding and mobilization advance recovery deductions.
- Net certified amount feeds into Phase 15 Supplier Invoice matching.
