# Energy Balance, DG Operations & Solar Generation

## Equation
$$\text{Total Energy Available} = \text{Grid Import} + \text{DG Generation} + \text{Solar Generation} - \text{Solar Export}$$
$$\text{Technical Loss} = \text{Total Energy Available} - \text{Metered Consumption}$$
- Tracks DG efficiency (kWh / litre of fuel) and solar self-consumption percentages.
