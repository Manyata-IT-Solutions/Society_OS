# Sustainability Metrics & Operational Carbon Accounting

## Metrics
- Water Intensity: $\text{KL} / \text{Occupied Unit}$
- Energy Intensity: $\text{kWh} / \text{Occupied Unit}$
- Solar Share %, Recycled Water Reuse %, Tanker Dependency %
- Estimated Operational Carbon Emissions: $\text{Activity Data} \times \text{Emission Factor}$
