# Safety & Compliance Bounded Context

## Subdomains
- **Emergency & SOS**: Distress signals, triage, panic alerts, mobile responder dispatch.
- **Incident & Command**: Severe incident management, command level, command handover, timeline.
- **Evacuation & Muster**: Zone-scoped evacuation orders, presence resolution, muster point accountability, self-safe reporting.
- **Investigations & CAPA**: 5-Whys root cause analysis, lessons learned, corrective/preventive actions, verifier closure.
- **Hazards & Risks**: Hazard registers, near-misses, 5x5 likelihood $\times$ impact matrix, risk controls.
- **Inspections & Drills**: Checklists, recurring inspection schedules, simulation drills, evaluation metrics.
- **Compliance & Credentials**: Statutory requirements, applicability assessments, obligation calendars, licenses/permits/NOCs, insurance readiness.
