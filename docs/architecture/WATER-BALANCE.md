# Water Balance, Tankers & Treatment Management

## Equation
$$\text{Total Freshwater Input} = \text{Municipal} + \text{Borewell} + \text{Tanker}$$
$$\text{Unaccounted Water} = \text{Total Freshwater Input} - \text{Metered Uses} - \Delta \text{Storage}$$
- Unaccounted water is marked as `POSSIBLE_LOSS` with completeness status without premature accusations.
