# Governance Meetings & Quorum Calculation Engine

## Overview
- **Meeting Types**: AGM, EGM, Committee Meetings, Special General Body Assemblies.
- **Venues**: Supports Physical, Online (external URL), and Hybrid attendance.
- **Quorum Snapshot**: Evaluates present verified attendees against frozen eligibility snapshots and quorum policy percentages (e.g. 25% statutory threshold).
- **Adjournment**: If quorum is not met, the meeting is marked `ADJOURNED` with historical status preserved without overwriting original records.
