# Hazard Register & Risk Matrix Architecture

## 5x5 Risk Matrix
$$\text{Inherent Risk Score} = \text{Likelihood (1–5)} \times \text{Impact (1–5)}$$
$$\text{Residual Risk Score} = \text{Residual Likelihood} \times \text{Residual Impact}$$
- Controls categorized into `PREVENTIVE`, `DETECTIVE`, `CORRECTIVE`, `RECOVERY`.
